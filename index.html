GOPISUNA VIDEO STUDIO

Free Online Video Editor

Create and edit video projects directly in your browser. GOPISUNA VIDEO STUDIO brings your media, timeline and editing controls together in one workspace, so you can start a project without installing a desktop editing application.

Edit on a multi-track timeline

Arrange video, images and audio on timeline tracks. Build your sequence by placing media, moving clips and refining the order of your project.

Trim, split and organize clips

Shape your edit with clip-level actions such as trimming, splitting, duplicating and deleting. Use the preview and timeline together while you work.

Work with different formats

Bring supported video, image and audio files into your project. Available import and export options are shown inside the editor.

Prepare videos for different screens

Choose a project aspect ratio for common formats such as landscape, vertical, square and classic video layouts.

How to edit a video online

  1. Open the editor at the top of this page.
  2. Import a video, image or audio file from your device.
  3. Arrange your clips on the timeline and make your edits.
  4. Preview your project, then use the Export controls to render your video.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to install video editing software?

The editor runs in a modern web browser. Open this page to access the editing workspace; your browser and device must support the media features used by the editor.

Can I edit video and audio in one project?

The workspace provides separate visual and audio timeline tracks. Import supported media and arrange the clips in your project.

Can I create vertical videos?

The project format controls include a vertical 9:16 option, alongside landscape, square and other aspect ratios.

How do I export my finished video?

Use the Export controls in the editor. Available rendering options depend on your browser and device capabilities.
