Video editing guide

How to Edit Videos Online

A clear video-editing workflow starts with a simple plan. This guide walks through the common steps you can follow in GOPISUNA VIDEO STUDIO, from importing media to preparing an export.

1. Prepare your media

Choose the video clips, images and audio you want to use. Keep the original files together so they are easy to find while you work. Check that your browser supports the media formats you plan to import.

2. Open the editor and import files

Open GOPISUNA VIDEO STUDIO and use the Import control to select supported video, image or audio files from your device. Wait for the media to appear in the project library before arranging it.

3. Arrange clips on the timeline

Place your visual clips in the order you want viewers to see them. Add audio to an audio track when needed. You can build the sequence in stages instead of trying to perfect every clip at once.

4. Trim and split your footage

Move the playhead to the point you want to adjust, select the relevant clip and use the available trim or split controls. Remove sections that do not support the story and keep transitions between shots easy to follow.

5. Preview the full sequence

Play the project from the beginning and watch for gaps, abrupt cuts, mismatched audio or clips that remain on screen too long. Make small timeline adjustments and preview again.

6. Choose export settings

Open the Export controls and select from the rendering options available in your browser. Export support and performance depend on your browser, device memory and the media you are editing. Keep the tab open while rendering.

A few practical editing tips

Use clear filenames, save a project copy as you work, avoid unnecessarily large source files on devices with limited memory, and preview the exported file before sharing it.
