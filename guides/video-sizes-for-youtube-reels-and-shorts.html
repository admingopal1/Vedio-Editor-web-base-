Best Video Sizes for YouTube, Reels, Shorts and TikTok

Choosing the right aspect ratio before you edit saves cropping and keeps important details inside the visible area.

YouTube (16:9)

Use 16:9 landscape, 1920 x 1080 for Full HD or 3840 x 2160 for 4K. This is the standard for long videos on TV and computers.

Shorts, Reels and TikTok (9:16)

Use 9:16 vertical at 1080 x 1920. Keep captions and faces away from the very top and bottom, where the app interface covers the video.

Instagram feed (4:5 or 1:1)

4:5 at 1080 x 1350 fills more of the phone screen than a square. Use 1:1 at 1080 x 1080 when you want a video that works everywhere.

Cinematic (21:9)

Ultrawide 21:9 gives a film look but adds black bars on most platforms, so use it for a deliberate style.

Set it up in the editor

Pick the aspect ratio and frame rate when you start a project. The Auto Reframe presets can resize an existing landscape project for Shorts, Reels or Instagram.
