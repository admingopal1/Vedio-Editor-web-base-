How to Remove Silence from a Video

Long pauses make a video feel slow. Removing silence is one of the fastest ways to tighten a tutorial, interview or voiceover without losing any content.

Why it works

Viewers decide in seconds whether to keep watching. Cutting gaps between sentences keeps the pace up and shortens the video, often by ten to twenty percent.

Using the Silences tool

Select the clip on the timeline and press Silences. The editor finds quiet sections in the audio and splits the clip around them so you can delete the gaps in one go.

Close the gaps

After deleting the silent parts, use Close gaps so the remaining clips join up without empty space. Ripple editing keeps later clips in sync.

Do not cut too hard

Leave a short pause, around a quarter of a second, between sentences. Cutting every breath sounds robotic. Play the result and add a quick fade or a cutaway where a jump feels abrupt.

Check the audio

Use the loudness normalise option afterwards so the volume stays even across the edited clips.
