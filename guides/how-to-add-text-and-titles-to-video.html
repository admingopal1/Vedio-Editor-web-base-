How to Add Text and Titles to a Video

Text explains what is on screen, introduces people and gives viewers a reason to keep watching.

Add a text clip

Open the Text tab, choose a style and add it to the timeline. Text lives on its own track, so you can move and resize it like any other clip.

Edit the look

Change the font, size, colour, background, outline and shadow in the inspector. Add an animation so the title does not just pop onto the screen.

Keep it readable

Use large text with strong contrast against the footage. A semi-transparent background box keeps words legible over busy scenes. Avoid more than two lines at a time.

Timing

Show each text clip long enough to read it twice. A good rule is about one second for every three words, plus a little extra.

Safe areas

For vertical video, keep text away from the edges so app buttons and captions do not cover it.
