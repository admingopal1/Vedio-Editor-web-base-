How to Make Slow Motion and Speed Ramp Videos

Changing speed is a simple way to add emphasis. Slow motion highlights a moment, fast motion saves time, and a speed ramp blends the two.

Constant speed change

Select a clip and set its speed in the inspector. Values below 1x slow it down and values above 1x speed it up. The clip length on the timeline updates to match.

Speed ramps

Use a speed ramp preset such as slow-mo dip or speed up to change speed smoothly during the clip instead of in one jump.

Get better slow motion

Footage shot at a higher frame rate, like 60 fps, looks smoothest when slowed. Standard 30 fps footage slowed too far can look choppy.

Match the audio

Speed changes also affect sound. Mute the clip or detach the audio and add music so slowed voices do not sound strange.

Time it to the beat

Place the ramp so the fast part lands just before the key action and the slow part covers the action itself.
