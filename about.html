About

GOPISUNA VIDEO STUDIO is a free online video editor that runs in your browser: no install, no account, and your files never leave your device.

What you can do

  • Multi-track timeline with video, audio, text and subtitle tracks
  • Trim, split, speed ramps, keyframes, transitions, filters and LUTs
  • Auto captions and subtitle import/export (SRT / VTT)
  • Vertical, square and widescreen projects for Shorts, Reels and YouTube
  • MP4 export and automatic project saving
