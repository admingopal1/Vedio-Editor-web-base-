Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Your videos stay on your device

The editor runs in your browser. The video, audio and image files you import are processed locally and are not uploaded to a server operated by this site. Your project is autosaved in your own browser storage (IndexedDB) so you can continue later. Clearing your browser data deletes it.

Information we collect

We do not require an account and do not ask for your name or email to use the editor. Our hosting provider (Vercel) may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and requested pages for security and operation.

Advertising and cookies

This site is supported by advertising. Our advertising partner (quge5.com and its network) may use cookies or similar identifiers to show ads, measure performance and limit repeat ads, and may ask permission to send browser notifications. You can decline notifications, block cookies in your browser settings, or turn off ad personalisation in your device or browser privacy settings. Third-party providers have their own privacy policies.

Optional features that download resources

The AI auto captions feature downloads a speech-recognition library and model from third-party servers (for example jsDelivr and Hugging Face) the first time you use it. Your audio is processed in your browser and is not sent to us.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions: see the Contact page.
